1. Motivation & Problem Definition

🎯 Controlling media with a mouse or keyboard is inconvenient while cooking or multitasking. We wanted a hands-free solution.
Motivation
Traditional media controls require physical interaction with input devices, making it difficult to control YouTube while your hands are occupied.
Problem Definition
Build a hands-free, AI-based gesture control system for YouTube — connecting data collection, AI modeling, and practical programming.

4. Experiments & Results

✅ Success Cases
System accurately triggers Play/Pause, Skip, and Mute with high confidence under normal lighting conditions.
⚠️ Failure Analysis
Confusion may occur in poor lighting or complex backgrounds. Resolved by collecting more diverse data under different lighting conditions.

5. Discussion & Applications

✅ Conclusion
Successfully developed a practical, AI-driven media controller that connects data curation, model training, and real-world programming.
⚠️ Limitations
Sensitive to extreme background changes and poor lighting environments. Confidence threshold at 95% may miss some gestures.
🚀 Future Improvements
Add more gestures (Volume Up/Down, Rewind). Improve model robustness against varied backgrounds and lighting.
💡 Applications
Hands-free media control for cooking, exercising, accessibility tools for people with limited mobility, and smart home integration.
